# Obelisk  
A self-evolving AI journal that records your progress, refines your blueprints, and remembers your creative evolution.
## About Obelisk

Every creator leaves behind fragments — ideas, revisions, and breakthroughs scattered across chats, code, and notebooks.  
Obelisk was built to bring that chaos into clarity.

It acts as a living book that grows with you — automatically recording your creative and technical progress, refining your blueprints, and preserving every important step of your journey.

Whether you're developing AI projects, writing, or building startups, Obelisk ensures your evolution is never lost — only documented, visualized, and ready for your next chapter.
## Features

🧠 **Progressive Chat System** — each chat session evolves independently, with automatic transitions to maintain workflow continuity.  
📚 **Session Independence** — every session stands alone; context is preserved only when the system auto-switches via progressive chat.  
🧾 **Blueprint Updates** — Obelisk refines and updates your creative or technical blueprints as you work.  
🎨 **Dynamic UI Flow** — clean, adaptable interface designed for clarity and focus.  
⚡ **Real-Time Recording** — captures insights, iterations, and project evolution as they happen.  
🔮 **Version Reflection (coming soon)** — visualize your growth over time, with snapshot comparisons of how each stage evolved.
